# Version 1 Default Assumptions

These are **illustrative values only**.

They are chosen to demonstrate that a ranking reversal can occur mathematically.
They are not estimates of real voter behavior.

## Young / First-Time Voters

- Cost per contact: $5.00
- Immediate turnout effect: 3 percentage points
- Candidate support probability: 55%
- Persistence parameter rho: 0.60

## Established Voters

- Cost per contact: $5.00
- Immediate turnout effect: 5 percentage points
- Candidate support probability: 55%
- Persistence parameter rho: 0.15

## Global

- beta: 0.95
- horizon: 4 future elections
- lambda: user-selected

## Why keep this file?

Every version of the project should distinguish:
1. model structure,
2. illustrative assumptions,
3. literature-calibrated empirical inputs.

That prevents a demonstration value from accidentally being presented as an
empirical finding.
